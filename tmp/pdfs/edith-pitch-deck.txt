===== SLIDE 5 =====
THE SOLUTION
One interface. Your context.
Your agents.
Example
You see a bug in UI and say: “Fix this in Codex and email me the summary.”
EDITH sees the screen, hears the request, recalls memory, plans the workflow, calls agents and executes.
HOW IT WORKS
01
Capture
Touch to speak and capture 
visual + audio context.
02
Context
Combine prompt, vision and 
semantic memory.
03
Planner
LLM planner decides what 
needs to happen.
04
Orchestration
Route task to Codex, Gmail 
or other agents.
05
Action
Complete the task and return 
the result.
Output: Codex drafts the fix. Gmail prepares the summary. EDITH reports back.
